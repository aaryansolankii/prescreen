Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026.

What we collect

  • What you give us: your email address, the name of the builder you used, your project files or a link to them, your store listing text, and any App Store review messages you paste in.
  • Payment details: handled entirely by the payment provider (PayPal, or a merchant-of-record checkout). We never see your card number.
  • Website visits: this site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard server information such as IP address and browser type; see GitHub's privacy statement. We do not run advertising trackers.

How we use it

  • To produce your report and answer your questions.
  • To email you about your report and, if you asked for one, a re-check. No newsletters unless you opt in.
  • In anonymised form (no names, app names or code) to improve the checklist.

AI processing

Parts of the review are produced with AI models from third-party providers (for example Anthropic's Claude or Google's Gemini). Extracts of your project configuration and text may be sent to those providers to generate findings. We use these services under their business terms, which do not use the content to train their models. Do not send us secrets; see the terms.

Where it is stored and for how long

  • Project files and extracted configuration are stored in a private cloud bucket and deleted within 7 days of your report (or sooner on request).
  • Your email address, the report itself and your rejection text are kept for 30 days after the last re-check so we can answer follow-up questions, then deleted.
  • We do not sell personal data and we do not share it with anyone except the providers named above and as required by law.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email aryan.solanki.stats@gmail.com and we will act within 7 days.

Children

This service is for people building apps and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes, and material changes will be emailed to anyone with an open report.
