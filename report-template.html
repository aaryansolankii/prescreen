Fix these first

Ordered by how likely each is to be cited, then by effort.

Live-site wrapper (4.2) fail · confidence High

What we found: capacitor.config.ts sets server.url to your production website, so the app loads your site in a WebView instead of bundling it.

Why reviewers cite it: Guideline 4.2 rejects apps that are not noticeably different from a website; Capacitor's own documentation says the live-URL mode is for development, not production.

How to fix: remove server.url, run your web build and npx cap sync so the assets ship inside the app; then add at least one capability a browser can't offer (push notifications tied to the user's data, camera or file access, offline use, a widget or share extension). Mention that capability in Notes for Review.

Permission strings (5.1.1(ii)) fail · confidence High

What we found: NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription is missing although the photo picker is used; NSCameraUsageDescription reads "This app needs camera access".

Why it matters: a missing string is rejected automatically at upload (ITMS-90683); boilerplate strings are cited under 5.1.1.

How to fix: in Info.plist (or ios.infoPlist in app.json for Expo) write one sentence per permission in your own words: "We use your camera to photograph receipts you want to track." Remove permissions your app never uses.

What Apple's upload check catches anyway

Missing purpose strings (ITMS-90683) and missing privacy manifests (ITMS-91053/91061) are rejected by App Store Connect at upload. If you get one of those emails, it is not a human rejection; fix the item and upload again.

Resubmission checklist

  1. Fix every fail.
  2. Fix every warn you reasonably can; say in Notes for Review what you changed.
  3. Paste the Notes for Review draft above into App Store Connect and fill the blanks.
  4. Reply to the previous rejection in the Resolution Center rather than opening a fresh submission, when Apple gives that option.
  5. Send us the new build or repo for your included re-check before you submit.
